Delete your Drill Down data

Drill Down by Snappy Yeti · Updated: October 9, 2026

Drill Down has no username or password. Your progress is saved on our game server (cloud save) and, if you connect it, linked to your Google Play Games account (Android) or Game Center (iPhone). You can delete this data at any time, either inside the game or by email. Deleting is permanent: your progress, coins, gems and drills cannot be restored afterwards.

1. Delete inside the game (fastest)

  1. Open Drill Down and tap the gear icon to open SETTINGS.
  2. Tap SAVE PROGRESS.
  3. At the bottom of that panel, tap DELETE MY DATA.
  4. Confirm twice: DELETE, then YES, DELETE.

Your phone needs an internet connection for this. When it is done, the game restarts as if it was just installed.

2. Delete by email (if you no longer have the game)

Send an email to trashygoo@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Drill Down data”. To help us find your data, include the Google Play Games (or Game Center) name your progress was linked to, if any. We will delete the data and confirm by email within 30 days.

3. What is deleted

  • your player record on our game server (Unity Gaming Services), including its link to Google Play Games or Game Center (your Google / Apple account itself is not touched);
  • your saved progress in the cloud (coins, gems, upgrades, drills, worlds and settings);
  • your entries on the in-game leaderboards;
  • the progress stored on your phone (when you delete inside the game);
  • the analytics identifier on your phone is reset, so new activity is no longer connected to the old one.

4. What may be kept

  • Purchase records. Google Play keeps the record of in-app purchases you made, as required by law and its own policies. Gems you bought, and everything you got with gems (for example drills or No Ads), are part of your progress and are deleted. They cannot be restored afterwards.
  • Analytics and crash reports. These are not linked to your name. They are removed automatically according to the retention periods described in our Privacy Policy.
  • Advertising data collected by Google AdMob is handled by Google under its own policies; you can reset your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.

Only want to start the game over? Uninstalling the game removes the progress on your phone, but the cloud copy stays (and comes back when you sign in with the same Google Play Games / Game Center account) until you use DELETE MY DATA.
