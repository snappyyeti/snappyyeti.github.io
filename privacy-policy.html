Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026 · Updated: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information our mobile game Drill Down (the “Game”) and other games by Snappy Yeti (“we”, “us”, “our”) handle, why, and the choices you have. We try to collect as little as possible: the Game has no username or password, no chat, and it never asks for your name, email address, age or location.

The data controller is Alexandru-Mihai Panait, trading as Snappy Yeti. Contact: trashygoo@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • Your progress is backed up to a game server so you can keep it on a new phone.
  • The weekly leaderboard shows a player name and your score to other players.
  • The Game shows ads from Google AdMob. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first.
  • We use Google Firebase to see how the Game is played and to find crashes.
  • Payments are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your card details.
  • You can delete your data inside the Game or by email (see section 6).

2. What leaves your device, and why

2.1 Player ID and cloud save (Unity Gaming Services)

When you first play, the Game creates a random, anonymous player ID with Unity Authentication (Unity Technologies). Your save data (progress, coins, gems, drills, items you own and settings) is stored with Unity Cloud Save under that ID, so it can be restored if you reinstall or change phones.

2.2 Google Play Games and Game Center (optional)

On Android you can connect the Game to Google Play Games. On iPhone, where available, the Game can use Apple Game Center. If you do, your Play Games or Game Center player ID is linked to your anonymous player ID, so the same progress can be found on another device. We do not receive your Google or Apple password or email address.

2.3 Leaderboard (public)

The weekly leaderboard uses Unity Leaderboards. It stores your player ID, a player name and your score (meters drilled this week). The name is generated automatically (for example “BraveDog#1234”) and you can change it in the Game. Your player name and score are visible to other players. Please do not use your real name or anything personal as your player name.

2.4 Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the Game is played (for example, where players get stuck), the Game sends gameplay events to Google Analytics for Firebase. These include runs started and finished, depth reached, worlds and bosses completed, upgrades bought, ads watched, offers shown, purchases started or completed, screens opened and short error messages. They come with device information (model, operating system, app version, country) and an app-instance identifier. We do not send your name, contact details or anything you type.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, analytics stays off until you agree to the relevant purposes in the consent form (section 3). If you refuse, no analytics events are sent.

2.5 Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the Game crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report: the technical error and where it happened in the code, device model, operating system, app version, a Crashlytics installation ID and basic context (for example which world and screen you were on). We use this only to find and fix bugs.

2.6 Game settings (Firebase Remote Config)

The Game downloads its tuning settings (for example, how often ads may appear) from Firebase Remote Config. To do this, Firebase receives a Firebase installation ID and basic app and device information. We sometimes test two versions of a setting with different groups of players; the group label is recorded with analytics.

2.7 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for an in-game reward, and occasional full-screen ads between runs. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (for example the Google Advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA if you allow tracking), your IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this to show ads, limit repeats, measure results, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, show more relevant ads. Ads in the Game are limited to content suitable for teenagers. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

2.8 In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. The store tells the Game which item was bought (product ID, price and currency, a purchase token or receipt) so it can be delivered. We never receive your payment card or bank details.

2.9 Notifications

If you allow notifications, the Game schedules local reminders on your device (for example, when your mine is full). They are created on the device and send no data to us. You can turn them off in your device settings.

3. Your consent and choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: when you start the Game, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform asks for your choices. Until you answer, analytics is off. You can refuse; you will then see limited, non-personalized ads, and no analytics is collected. You can change your choice at any time with PRIVACY OPTIONS in the Game's settings.
  • iPhone: where the Game asks to track you for ads (Apple's App Tracking Transparency), you can refuse. You can change this later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (for example Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • California and other US states: you can opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising using the options above, or by contacting us.

5. How long we keep data

  • Cloud save, player ID and leaderboard entry: until you delete your data (section 6), or until we shut the Game down.
  • Analytics events: 14 months in Google Analytics, then deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports: for the limited period set by Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Advertising data: kept by Google under its own policies.
  • Emails you send us: as long as needed to answer you.

6. Deleting your data

In the Game: open SETTINGS → SAVE PROGRESS → DELETE MY DATA and confirm. This deletes your cloud save, your leaderboard entry and your player ID, resets the analytics data on the device and wipes the progress on the phone. It cannot be undone. If you no longer have the Game, email us. Full instructions: Delete your Drill Down data.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has used the Game and you want their data removed, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU), you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive a copy of it, and to withdraw your consent at any time (withdrawing does not affect processing done before). To use these rights, email us. Because the Game has no account with your name, include the Google Play Games (or Game Center) name your progress was linked to, if any, so we can find your data. We answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority; in Romania this is the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP).

9. Where data is processed

Google and Unity may process data outside your country, including in the United States. They do so under the safeguards they describe in their own privacy terms.

10. Security

Data sent by the Game travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We use established service providers and keep the Game's software up to date. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.
