This Privacy Policy explains how Snappy Yeti (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information when you play our mobile games, including Dig Down (the “Game”). The data controller is LEGAL_NAME. If you have any questions, contact us at CONTACT_EMAIL.

1. Information we collect ourselves

The Game does not require an account and we do not ask for your name, email address or other contact details. Your game progress (such as your best depth, coins, upgrades and settings) is stored only on your device. We do not receive or store this information on our servers. If you uninstall the Game, this progress is deleted.

2. Advertising

The Game is free to play and shows advertisements. Some ads are optional: you can choose to watch them in exchange for an in-game reward. Ads are served through AppLovin MAX and the advertising networks it works with (our “advertising partners”). To deliver and measure ads, our advertising partners may automatically collect information from your device, such as:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (for example, the Google Advertising ID);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device type, operating system, language, and app version;
  • information about the ads you see and interact with.

This information is used to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and, where permitted, show ads that are more relevant to you. Our advertising partners process this information under their own privacy policies, for example the AppLovin Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices and consent

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before personalized ads are shown, the Game asks for your consent. You can refuse; you will then see non-personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy option in the Game, where available.
  • All Android users: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • California and other US states: you can opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising using the options above or by contacting us.

4. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

5. Data retention and security

We do not keep personal data on our own servers. Data collected by our advertising partners is kept according to their policies. We choose reputable partners and keep the Game’s software up to date to protect your information.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not store personal data ourselves, most requests are best handled by our advertising partners, but you can always contact us and we will help. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the effective date shown at the top.
