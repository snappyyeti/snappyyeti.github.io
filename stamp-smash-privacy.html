Stamp Smash Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information our mobile game Stamp Smash: Monster Puzzle (the “Game”) handles, why, and the choices you have. We collect as little as possible: the Game has no account, no username or password, no chat, no cloud save and no leaderboard, and it never asks for your name, email address or location.

The data controller is Alexandru-Mihai Panait, trading as Snappy Yeti. Contact: trashygoo@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • Your progress (levels, stars, collection, settings) is saved only on your device.
  • The Game asks for your birth year once. It stays on your device and is used only to decide whether ads may be personalized (no personalized ads under 16).
  • The Game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay, which selects ads from ad networks such as Google AdMob, ironSource Ads and Unity Ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first.
  • With your consent, we use GameAnalytics to see how the Game is played.
  • Payments (“Remove Ads”) are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your card details.

2. What leaves your device, and why

2.1 Advertising (Unity LevelPlay and its ad networks)

The Game is free and shows ads: optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for an in-game reward (for example +3 mallets), occasional full-screen ads between levels, and a small banner. Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies / ironSource), which runs an auction between ad networks. The networks currently used are Google AdMob, ironSource Ads and Unity Ads; if we add another network, we will list it here.

To show and measure ads, LevelPlay and these networks may collect your device's advertising identifier (the Google Advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA if you allow tracking), your IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. They use this to show ads, limit repeats, measure results, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, show more relevant ads. If you are under 16, or if you did not give consent, only non-personalized ads are requested. Ads in the Game are limited to content suitable for teenagers.

Learn more: Unity privacy policy, Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

2.2 Analytics (GameAnalytics)

To understand how the Game is played (for example which levels are too hard and where players stop), the Game sends gameplay events to GameAnalytics: levels started, won or lost, moves and helpers used, session length, ads offered or watched, purchases, and the Game's difficulty settings for you. They come with a random installation ID created by the Game, device information (model, operating system, app version, country) and the test group you are in when we compare two versions of a setting. We do not send your name, contact details, birth year or anything you type.

Analytics is sent only after you consent in the consent form (section 3). If you refuse, or if you are under 16, no analytics events leave your device.

2.3 Game settings

The Game may download its tuning settings (for example how often ads may appear) as a small file from our website (hosted by GitHub). Like any web request, this reveals your IP address to the host. No other data is sent.

2.4 Crash reports

If the Game crashes, Google Play or Apple may share technical crash and performance reports with us through their developer consoles, depending on your device settings. These contain no information that identifies you directly.

2.5 In-app purchases

“Remove Ads” is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. The store tells the Game which item was bought (product ID, price and currency, a purchase token or receipt) so it can be delivered and restored. We never receive your payment card or bank details.

2.6 Notifications

If you allow notifications, the Game schedules local reminders on your device (for example when your daily reward is ready). They are created on the device and send no data to us. You can turn them off in your device settings.

2.7 Sharing results

When you tap SHARE (for example on the Daily Smash result), the Game opens your device's share menu with a text and an image of your result. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share with.

3. Your consent and choices

  • Birth year: asked once when you first start the Game; stored only on your device.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform asks for your choices before any ad or analytics request. You can refuse; you will then see limited, non-personalized ads, and no analytics is collected. You can change your choice at any time with Ad privacy choices in the Game's settings.
  • iPhone: where the Game asks to track you for ads (Apple's App Tracking Transparency), you can refuse. You can change this later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (for example Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • California and other US states: you can opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising using the options above, or by contacting us.

5. How long we keep data

  • Progress and settings: on your device until you uninstall the Game.
  • Analytics events: kept by GameAnalytics under its retention settings, then deleted.
  • Advertising data: kept by Unity and the ad networks under their own policies.
  • Emails you send us: as long as needed to answer you.

6. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the Game deletes your progress, settings and birth year from your device. Analytics data is linked only to a random installation ID; to have it deleted, email us and we will remove what can be linked to you. You can also reset your advertising ID in your device settings (section 3).

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Players who tell us they are under 16 only receive non-personalized ads and send no analytics. If you believe a child has used the Game and you want their data removed, contact us.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU), you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive a copy of it, and to withdraw your consent at any time (withdrawing does not affect processing done before). To use these rights, email us. We answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority; in Romania this is the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP).

9. Where data is processed

Unity, Google, GameAnalytics and the ad networks may process data outside your country, including in the United States. They do so under the safeguards they describe in their own privacy terms.

10. Security

Data sent by the Game travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We use established service providers and keep the Game's software up to date. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The latest version is always on this page, with the date shown at the top.
